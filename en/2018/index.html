GeoNord 2018 - Sunset (GC71DDD)


Sunset, the fourth edition of GeoNord events, took place in Dunkirk and Leffrinckouke from July 12 to 15, 2018, with the theme of fire. What was supposed to be the last edition was very emotional for the members of the organization. Twenty Labcaches, VIP packs packed with goodies and sold online within hours of release, the appearance of collector’s boxes of the four elements “made in GeoNord,” the secret mission of the travel tags, a visit to the fort in the dunes, more than 2,500 people… everything was there, even the sun, which visited us for the fourth time.

More than 1,000 people attended, and your smiles over the years have motivated the team to continue writing the history of GeoNord events in 2022 with surprises never seen before!
