In July 2016, we headed to the forest of Saint Amand Les Eaux for the MEGA event Retour aux Sources (Back to the Roots), focused on the theme of water and bringing together 2,000 people of 14 different nationalities.

Retour aux Sources is the very first GeoNord event to obtain MEGA status. The success of the Labcaches and the creation of the first 3D geocoin attracted everyone's attention, not to mention the VIP packs, which sold out in just a few days. This second edition was not only bigger in terms of participants, but also in terms of excitement.
