1. **GPS Maze Adventures, what is that?** A [GPS MAZE](https://www.geocaching.com/about/cache_types.aspx) is a special and rare type of event-related cache. This is a traveling educational exhibit in the shape of a maze designed to teach people of all ages about GPS technology and Geocaching. The Maze will offer you to immerse yourself in the history of geocaching through a playful maze. During the visit of the exhibition, participate in the activities, solve riddles and don’t miss the opportunity find the 10 special Maze Labcaches.

2. **What can we found in this maze?** Explanatory panels on the types of caches, information on trackables, how to make a “night-cache”, learn about T5 caches in complete safety, interactive elements, etc., everything you always wanted to know about Geocaching as well as more surprises!

3. **Will we get the “GPS Maze” cache icon after visiting the exhibition?** Yes, this icon will be assigned to you after you have logged your “Attended” on the GPS Maze listing.

4. **Do I need anything to visit the exhibition?** No, just a little patience to get in line (if there is any queue) before you can access the Maze.

5. **Will there be labcaches?** Yes, and aventure of 10 special Maze Labcaches will be available inside the exhibition.

6. **Will the exhibition be in French only?** No, although most of the information will be in French, some panels and info will also be translated into English. Non-French-speaking people can get a guidebook in English at the entrance of the exhibition.

7. **When can we visit the exhibition?** The dates and times of opening of our GPS MAZE are on the event listing page on the Geocaching website.
