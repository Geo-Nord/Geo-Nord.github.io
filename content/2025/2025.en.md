In 2025, the GPS MAZE returns to France, and it is in the heart of Champagne in partnership with the Geocachers of Champagne that we will invite you to discover an ever more fun and interactive exhibition full of new interactive games and surprises!

The GPS Adventures Maze exhibition will be open to the public the weeked on May 31st, 2025 and it will takes place near the Block Party Event [GeoCircus](https://coord.info/GCANGAA).
