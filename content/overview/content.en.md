At the end of 2014, we launched the GeoNord adventure based on the theme of the four elements. Between 2015 and 2018, we organized three to four days of events each year to promote the heritage of the Nord-Pas-De-Calais region. What was supposed to be a fairly large gathering each year eventually became an unmissable event for French and foreign geocachers alike. It was so successful that each year we broke our own record for the largest geocaching event ever organized in France!

After a break of a few years and the COVID-19 pandemic, we returned in 2022 for a 5-day weekend for a MEGA event and the very first GPS MAZE ADVENTURES in France, entirely designed from scratch by us.

Since then, we have been offering other MEGA event organizing teams the opportunity to host the GPS MAZE as well.
