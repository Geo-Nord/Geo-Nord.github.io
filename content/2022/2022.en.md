After celebrating the four elements (earth, water, air, and fire) for four years in a row and taking a well-deserved break, we are back for a new chapter from July 13 to 17, 2022, at Olhain Departmental Park!

For the occasion, the GeoNord team is preparing five days filled with activities, geocaching, and events. As usual, we are creating beautiful products, geocoins, T-shirts, and entry packs that are affordable for as many people as possible. We are privatizing the entire campground and park accommodations to host many participants in the “Signal Village.” We are planning activities for as many people as possible and offering discounted ticket packs for park activities such as zip-lining, four-season sledding, mini-golf, suspended nets, and even the swimming pool.

Finally, GeoNord, in collaboration with Geocaching HQ, is building a new GPS MAZE ADVENTURES exhibition to offer you the opportunity to visit the GPS Maze exhibition for the first time in France. Many geocachers will be making the trip so as not to miss the opportunity to be with us and get the new icon!


To this date, [GeoNord Is a Great Adventure](https://coord.info/GC80000) still holds the attendance record for the biggest ever Geocaching event in France, thank you to all the participants!
